How PARK-MATE Works

Reserve your LPU campus parking spot in seconds before you even reach the university gate.

01

Sign In with LPU ID

Log in using your University Registration ID or register a new campus account with your vehicle details.

02

Pick Block 15–35

Check live slot availability across all academic blocks and filter by Two-Wheeler or Four-Wheeler bays.

03

Select Slot & Confirm

Choose a green slot on the interactive lot map, set your arrival time & duration, and book for free (₹0.00).

04

Scan QR & Park

Use your instant Gate QR Pass at the automated barrier and navigate straight to your reserved bay.
