Campus Parking Regulations & Safety Guidelines

Lovely Professional University Campus Guidelines:
  1. Vehicles must only occupy designated spaces within their authorized reservation timeframe.
  2. Vehicles must be parked strictly between marked line bays without encroaching adjacent lanes.
  3. Campus speed limit is strictly 20 km/h. Drivers must yield to pedestrian walkways at all times.
  4. Automated barrier gate passes must be scanned via QR code upon both entry and departure.
  5. Valuables should not be left unattended; the University assumes no liability for personal items.
  6. Unauthorized overnight parking or blocking emergency lanes will incur estate disciplinary action.
